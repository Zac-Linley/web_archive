## ⌨️ Visual Keyboard — Your Productivity Accelerator

[](#️-visual-keyboard--your-productivity-accelerator)

Say goodbye to layers of clicks — manage your browser with a familiar keyboard layout.

**Bookmark Keyboard**

**Command Keyboard**

**What**

Display bookmarks on your new tab in a real keyboard layout

Execute browser commands on any page via modifier + key combos

**How**

Open a new tab, press the corresponding key to open a site

Set a modifier combo (e.g. `Ctrl+Shift`), press any key to trigger

**Configurable**

Key mappings, keyboard layout, themes

Command bindings, modifier combos, domain black/whitelists

**Use Case**

Quick access to your favorite sites from the new tab

Tab management, page actions, scroll control — no mouse needed
